# Bench card

Tape this next to the shoe. If the stamp or the bender card disagrees with a row, cross the row out and write the shoe's number.

## Shoe

| Mark | Set it on |
| --- | --- |
| Arrow | Stub mark. Each offset mark. Each saddle wing. |
| Star | The back-to-back mark, measured from the back of the first 90. |
| Teardrop | The center mark of a three-point saddle. |
| Degree line | Where you stop the pull. |

Foot stays on the pedal for the whole pull. Hands stay out of the shoe.

## Stub — subtract take-up

Mark = stub height − take-up. Arrow on the mark. Pull to 90°.

| EMT | Take-up, if the stamp matches |
| --- | --- |
| 1/2 in. | 5 in. |
| 3/4 in. | 6 in. |
| 1 in. | 8 in. |

The shortest stub this shoe can make is the take-up itself.

## Offset — add shrink, then step back

Far mark = distance to the near edge + shrink. Near mark = far mark − (depth × multiplier). Arrow on both. Same angle. Flip 180° between them.

| Angle | Multiplier | Shrink per inch | A 4 in. rise, spacing | A 4 in. rise, shrink |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10° | 6 | 1/16 | 24 in. | 1/4 in. |
| 22.5° | 2.6 | 3/16 | 10-1/2 in. | 3/4 in. |
| 30° | 2 | 1/4 | 8 in. | 1 in. |
| 45° | 1.4 | 3/8 | 5-5/8 in. | 1-1/2 in. |
| 60° | 1.2 | 1/2 | 4-3/4 in. | 2 in. |

30° is the default. Use a steeper angle when the spacing will not fit.

## Three-point saddle

Depth is the height you have to clear. Center of the obstacle, then:

- Move the center mark ahead by depth × 3/16.
- Wings sit depth × 2-1/2 on each side of that center mark.
- Teardrop on the center mark, pull to 45°.
- Arrow on each wing, pull to 22.5°. Roll 180° between the wings.

A 2 in. saddle whose center is 15 in. out: center mark at 15-3/8 in., wings at 10-3/8 in. and 20-3/8 in.

## Four-point saddle

Two 30° offsets. Spacing = depth × 2. Shrink = depth × 1/4, applied to the first offset. The flat equals the width of the obstacle. Do not put shrink into the flat.

## Bend budget

Count every degree between pull points. The cap in 358.26 is 360°.

| This bend | Spends |
| --- | --- |
| 90° | 90° |
| 30° offset | 60° |
| 45° offset | 90° |
| Three-point saddle | 90° |
| Four-point saddle at 30° | 120° |
| 10° kick or box offset | 10° or 20° |

A box or a conduit body resets the count. Read 358.24, 358.26, 358.28, and 358.30 from the book on the bench.
